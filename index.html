LunaGlow: where should a skincare brand spend its next euro?

A 90-day marketing analytics simulation for a direct-to-consumer skincare brand advertising on Meta, Google and TikTok. I built the data model, the analysis and the dashboard end to end.

The data is synthetic (modelled GA4 and ad-platform exports), so the findings show my method, not a real company's results.

95%

gap in customer acquisition cost (CAC) between Google and TikTok

+17%

modelled conversion lift from reallocating the budget

How it works

  1. Synthetic data90 days of GA4, Meta, Google and TikTok data
  2. PostgreSQLKPI views that compare channels on the same terms
  3. Python and pandasCleaning, analysis and the reallocation model
  4. Power BIA dark-theme dashboard that shows the gap at a glance

n8n handles the automation around it. The project grew out of an earlier end-to-end pipeline: three Kaggle datasets, PostgreSQL KPI views, a two-page Power BI dashboard and a weekly n8n refresh.
